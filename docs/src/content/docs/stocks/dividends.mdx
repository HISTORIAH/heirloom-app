Tokenized stocks don't pay dividends in cash or mint new tokens for a split. Both work
through a **multiplier** on the token. Your wallet shows your balance times the
multiplier:

```txt
balance        10.00000000
multiplier     1.0032690125
wallet shows   10.032690125
```

- A **dividend** nudges the multiplier up by the dividend's value, reinvested.
- A **split** multiplies it, by 2 for a 2-for-1 split. A reverse split divides it.

Your underlying balance never changes. The issuer sets each new multiplier and its date
in advance, so the dashboard lists your **upcoming corporate actions**: which stocks
change, by how much, and when.

## Why backups use a share

Heirloom always moves the underlying balance, so every transfer stays exact. It is also
why backup mode covers a **share** of a holding rather than a number of shares: "10
shares" would mean something different after each dividend, while "50%" never does.
Amounts you type in the app are in the units your wallet shows.
