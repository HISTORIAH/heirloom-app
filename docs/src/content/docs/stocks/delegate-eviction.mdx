Backup mode works by approving your plan to move a token account. A token account can
have only **one** approval at a time, so approving anything else on it, like a DEX order
or a lending deposit, replaces Heirloom's. Nothing warns you. The holding just stops
being recoverable.

## What breaks it

- Approving another app or wallet on the same token account
- Revoking approvals in your wallet's security settings
- Closing the token account, for example after selling everything

Trading part of the position or buying more does **not** break it.

## How you find out

The dashboard checks every covered holding against your plan. A broken one shows
**Coverage lost**, with a banner saying how many are affected.

## How to fix it

Press **Re-approve** on the holding. One transaction covers it again at the same share.
If you sold out and closed the account, press **Remove record** instead.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you approve apps often">
For stocks you plan to hold untouched, [vault mode](/docs/stocks/overview/#vault-mode)
can't be broken this way.
</Callout>

## CPI Guard

Some wallets offer CPI Guard, which stops programs acting on your account. While it is
on, you can't cover a new holding. Turning it on after a holding is covered is fine:
recovery still works, and you can still stop covering.
