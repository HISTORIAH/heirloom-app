Backup mode works by making your plan the **delegate** of each covered token account. SPL
Token allows exactly **one** delegate per account. So if you later approve something else on
that account — a DEX order, a lending deposit — the new approval **replaces** Heirloom's.

Nothing fails when that happens. There is no error and no warning from your wallet. The
position just stops being recoverable, and a recovery attempt would be refused with
`DelegateEvicted`.

## What does not cause it

- **Trading.** Selling or sending part of a covered position doesn't touch the approval.
  Token-2022 only reduces an allowance when the *delegate* spends from it, never when you
  do, so an unlimited approval survives any amount of trading.
- **Receiving more.** Buying more of a covered stock into the same account is covered
  automatically, at the same share.

## What does

- Approving another program or wallet on the same token account.
- Revoking approvals in your wallet's security settings.
- Closing the token account, for example after selling out.

## How you find out

The dashboard reads every covered account's current delegate and allowance and compares
them against your plan. An evicted holding shows as **Coverage lost**, and a banner at the top
of the dashboard says how many are affected. The portfolio marks it the same way.

## How to fix it

Press **Re-approve** on the holding. It is one transaction that retires the old coverage
record and covers the holding again, approving the plan once more at the same share.

If you sold out and closed the account, press **Remove record** instead. It tidies up the
plan — a plan can only be closed once nothing is left covered.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you approve apps often">
Every approval is a chance to evict the backup. For stocks you mean to hold untouched for a
long time, a [vault](/docs/stocks/overview/#vault-mode) can't be displaced at all.
</Callout>

## CPI Guard

Some wallets offer **CPI Guard**, a Token-2022 setting that stops programs from acting on
your account on your behalf. While it is on, a holding can't be *covered*, because the
approval is made through the program. Once covered, turning it on does no harm: recovery
moves tokens as the delegate, which CPI Guard allows, and you can still stop covering.
