| Instruction | Signer | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `initialize` | Owner | Opens an estate, optionally with a first token, and moves the opening deposit in |
| `register_asset` | Owner | Adds a new token to an estate, with a deposit |
| `update_field` | Owner or check-in signer | Checks in. Timing changes apply only when the owner signs |
| `update_heir` | Owner | Moves the estate to a new heir, one asset per call |
| `claim` | Heir | Pays one asset to the heir, minus 0.75% |

| `withdraw_protected_lulo` | Owner | Recalls a protected position |

| `complete_withdraw_regular_lulo` | Owner | Finishes a boosted withdrawal |

## Rules to know

### update_field

Always records the check-in, then applies any timing arguments, but only if the owner
signed. Pass all three as `null` for a plain check-in. Timing sent by the check-in signer
is ignored without an error.

### claim and revoke

Each call moves one asset. The SOL call closes the estate, so put every token call first
and the SOL call last, in one transaction. Otherwise it fails with `TokensFirst`. Both fail
with `FundsStillDeployed` while the asset is in Lulo.

### update_heir

One asset per call. Run every call to the end, or assets are left split across two
estates. It fails with `EstatePaused` during a guardian pause.

### delegate_defer

Works once, and only before the estate is claimable. Otherwise it fails with
`AlreadyDeferred` or `DeferWindowExpired`.

### initialize

The guardian (`delegate`) and check-in signer (`check_in_signer`) are optional accounts.
Leave either out and that role stays empty for the life of the estate. Leave out the
mint accounts for a SOL-only estate.
