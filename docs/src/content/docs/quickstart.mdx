You need a Solana wallet, a little SOL for network fees, and the address of the wallet
that should inherit.

<Callout type="note" title="You are on devnet">
Set your wallet to devnet and get test SOL from a faucet first. See
[Addresses & clusters](/docs/reference/addresses/).
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet">
    Open [app.heirlm.xyz](https://app.heirlm.xyz/dashboard) and connect. This wallet
    becomes the estate's owner, the only wallet that can change it or take assets back,
    so connect the one you want to own it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name an heir">
    Paste the heir's wallet address. One estate has one heir. To leave different things
    to different people, open one estate each.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add assets">
    Pick SOL, tokens, or both, and how much of each. You can add more later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the timing">
    - **Check-in interval:** how long you can go without checking in. 30 days is common.
    - **Grace period:** extra time after a missed check-in before the heir can claim.

    Optionally name a guardian and a check-in signer. See
    [who can do what](/docs/how-it-works/#who-can-do-what).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and sign">
    One transaction creates the estate and moves your assets in. The clock starts when
    it lands.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check in">
    Press check in on the dashboard whenever the countdown gets low. It costs only a
    network fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>
