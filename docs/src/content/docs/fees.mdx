| Action | Fee | Charged on |
|---|---|---|
| Open an estate, add assets, check in | Free | |
| Change the heir, pause as a guardian | Free | |
| Heir claims | **0.75%** | Each asset paid out |
| Owner closes the estate | **0.5%** | Each asset returned |
| Yield | **10%** | The yield only, never the principal |

Every action also costs a Solana network fee, a fraction of a cent. Fees go to the
Heirloom treasury when assets leave the estate.

Rent is not a fee. Every account an estate creates holds a little SOL to stay alive on
Solana, and you get it back when the estate closes. The app leaves it out of the balance
it shows, which is why an estate funded with 1 SOL shows slightly less.

## Examples

- An heir claiming 100 USDC receives 99.25 USDC.
- An owner closing an estate with 10 SOL gets back 9.95 SOL, plus the rent.
- A Lulo position that put in 1,000 USDC and returned 1,050 USDC pays 5 USDC: 10% of
  the 50 USDC earned. A position that returns the same or less pays nothing.

## Details

- Fees are rounded up to the nearest base unit, so on any normal balance the difference
  is at most one unit.
- The rates and the treasury address are fixed in the program. Changing them would
  require deploying a new program; no transaction can change them.
- Closing costs less than claiming on purpose, so taking your own assets back is never
  the expensive option.

The exact constants are in [Addresses & clusters](/docs/reference/addresses/#protocol-constants).
