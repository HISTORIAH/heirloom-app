Assets waiting in an estate can earn. Heirloom can move them into
[Lulo](https://lulo.fi) lending and back whenever you want. Principal and yield return
to the estate, never to a wallet. Heirloom takes **10% of the yield** and nothing from
the principal.

## Two pools

| Pool | What it is | Getting it back |
|---|---|---|
| **Protected** | Lower rate, principal protection | One step, immediate |
| **Boosted** | Higher rate, no protection | Two steps: request, then complete |

You choose per asset.

<Callout type="danger" title="Recall before you stop checking in">
An asset in a Lulo position can't be claimed, closed or moved to a new heir, and only
the owner can recall it. If you stop checking in with funds still in Lulo, your heir
can't get them out. Recall your positions if you stop using an estate actively.
</Callout>

## The fee

When funds come back, everything up to what you put in counts as principal and is
untouched. Only the amount above that is yield, and Heirloom takes 10% of it. A position
that comes back flat or down pays nothing.

## Risk

Using Lulo means taking on Lulo's risk, including its exposure to Kamino, for as long as
the position is open. Heirloom does not insure it.

## In the app

The program's Lulo support is live. The app's yield screens are a preview: the rates are
placeholders and the buttons are not yet connected to the program. Until they are, use
the [instructions](/docs/developers/instructions/) directly. The preview lists USDC, USDT and
PYUSD.

Staking estate SOL with a validator is on the roadmap. The program does not support it
yet.
