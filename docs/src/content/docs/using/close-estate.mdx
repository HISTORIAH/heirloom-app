Closing an estate returns everything in it to your wallet and ends the estate. You can
do it at any time before the heir claims, including after the estate has become
claimable. Your own assets are never out of reach.

## The fee

**0.5%** of each asset. You also get back the rent the estate's accounts were holding.

## Before you close

- **Recall any yield first.** Assets in a Lulo position can't leave, and closing fails
  with `FundsStillDeployed`. For a boosted position, both steps of the withdrawal must
  finish. See [Earn yield](/docs/using/yield/).
- **Closing is final.** The timing, guardian, check-in signer and the heir's claim all
  go with it. To keep protecting the assets, you have to open a new estate.

## If the estate is already claimable

You and the heir can both act. There is no lock: whoever's transaction lands first wins,
and the other one fails.

## Reopening for the same heir

Right after closing, creating a new estate for the same heir can fail for a few seconds
while the network catches up. The app retries for up to 20 seconds; if it still fails,
wait a moment and try again.
