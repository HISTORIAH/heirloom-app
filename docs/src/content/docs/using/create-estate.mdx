Creating an estate is one transaction. The app's wizard collects four screens of input
and then sends `initialize` — plus one `register_asset` per additional token — as a
single atomic instruction bundle.

Open [app.heirlm.xyz/create-vault](https://app.heirlm.xyz/create-vault) with a wallet
connected.

## Step 1 — Heir

| Field | On-chain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Heir address | `heir` | A raw address. It is a PDA seed, so it fixes the estate's identity |
| Label | `label` | Up to 32 bytes, public, purely for your own bookkeeping |
| Guardian *(optional)* | `delegate` | May defer the deadline once. Cannot move funds |

<Callout type="warning" title="Check the heir address twice">
There is no name resolution and no undo. If you mistype it, the estate belongs to a
wallet nobody controls, and your only exit is an emergency withdraw. Paste from the
wallet, and consider sending a small transfer first to confirm it arrives.
</Callout>

## Step 2 — Deposit

Choose an opening SOL amount, any SPL tokens, or both. Every token you pick here is
registered, which creates its `AssetRecord` marker and the vault's token account in this
same transaction.

You are not committing to a final amount. Assets can be added and topped up at any time
— see [Fund an estate](/docs/using/fund-estate/).

## Step 3 — Heartbeat

| Field | On-chain | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Heartbeat interval | `heartbeat_interval` | 365 days |
| Grace period | `grace_period` | 365 days |
| Pause duration | `pause_duration` | 365 days |
| Hot signer *(optional)* | `hb_signer` | — |

The first two define the deadline; the third is how much time a guardian may add. All
three are editable later, and editing them counts as a check-in.

A **hot signer** is a second wallet of yours that may send check-ins and nothing else. If
the wallet that owns the estate is a hardware or cold wallet, register one — it is the
difference between a monthly ritual with a Ledger and tapping a button on your phone.

## Step 4 — Review

The review screen is the estate as it will exist. Read the two durations back to yourself
in days, confirm the heir, and sign.

## What the transaction does

<Code caption="One transaction, in order">
```txt
initialize(heartbeat_interval, grace_period, pause_duration, amount, label)
  ├─ create estate PDA          seeds ["estate", authority, heir]
  ├─ create vault PDA           seeds ["vault",  authority, heir]
  ├─ create asset record        seeds ["asset", estate, mint]      (if a mint was given)
  ├─ create vault token account                                    (if a mint was given)
  ├─ transfer the token deposit into the vault
  └─ transfer the SOL deposit into the vault

register_asset(amount)   ×N     one per additional token
```
</Code>

Because it is one transaction, it either all happens or none of it does. There is no
state where an estate exists but its funding failed.

## After it lands

The clock starts immediately — `last_heartbeat` is set to the transaction's timestamp,
not to zero. Your estate appears on the dashboard as **active**, with a countdown to the
grace window.

<Callout type="note" title="Re-creating an estate you just closed">
The estate PDA is derived from your address and your heir's, so closing one and
immediately reopening the same pair can fail while RPC snapshots still show the old
account. The app polls for up to 20 seconds and retries before surfacing
`Prior estate/vault PDAs not yet cleared on-chain.` — wait a few seconds and try again.
</Callout>

## Common failures

| Error | Cause |
|---|---|
| `IntervalTooLong` | A duration above 365 days |
| `IntervalNegative` | A negative duration |
| `LabelTooLong` | Label over 32 bytes — note bytes, not characters |
| `ZeroDepositAmount` | A registration with an amount of zero |
