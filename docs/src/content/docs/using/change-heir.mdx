The estate's address is derived from your wallet and the heir's, so changing the heir
moves everything to a **new estate** for the new heir and closes the old one. Assets go
straight from one estate to the other and never pass through a wallet.

## What carries over

Your timing, your last check-in, your guardian and your check-in signer all carry over.
Your clock is not restarted, so changing the heir neither buys nor costs you time. Any
guardian pause is cleared. It is free apart from network fees.

The old heir is not notified. They had nothing to lose unless the estate was already
claimable.

## Before you start

- **The estate can't be paused.** If a guardian has paused it, wait for the pause to
  end, check in once, then change the heir. Otherwise it fails with `EstatePaused`.
- **Recall any yield first.** Assets in a Lulo position can't move. See
  [Earn yield](/docs/using/yield/).
- **The new heir can't already have an estate from you.** One owner and heir pair can
  have only one estate.

## Change the heir or start over?

Changing the heir keeps your clock, timing and assets in place, with no fee. Open a new
estate instead only if you want to change your guardian or check-in signer, which
changing the heir carries over as they are.
