A check-in is a transaction that tells the program you are still here. It pushes the
deadline forward by your full interval, moves no funds, and costs only a network fee.

## The deadline

Your estate becomes claimable at your last check-in, plus your check-in interval, plus
your grace period. With a 30-day interval and a 7-day grace, the heir can claim 37 days
after your last check-in.

The two numbers mean different things. Miss the interval and you are late: the estate
enters grace and the app warns you. Only when grace also runs out can the heir claim.
That gap is your chance to be reminded, and your guardian's chance to step in.

## How to check in

- **From the dashboard.** Open the estate and press check in.
- **From your phone, with a check-in signer.** If the owner wallet is a hardware wallet,
  name a [check-in signer](/docs/how-it-works/#who-can-do-what) and check in with it at
  [app.heirlm.xyz/check-in](https://app.heirlm.xyz/check-in). Enter your address and
  your heir's, and sign. The signer can check in and do nothing else.
- **From a script.** A check-in is the `update_field` instruction with no settings. See
  [Clients](/docs/developers/clients/#check-in).

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't automate away the point">
An estate a script keeps alive forever never reaches your heir. If you automate
check-ins, make the automation depend on something only you can keep going, like a
device you carry or a bill you pay.
</Callout>

## What counts

| Counts as a check-in | Does not |
|---|---|
| Checking in | Adding assets |
| Changing the interval, grace or pause length | Earning or recalling yield |
| | Changing the heir |

## Choosing your timing

- **Short interval:** your heir gets access sooner when it matters, and you check in
  more often.
- **Long interval:** less to think about, and a longer wait for your heir.

Most people start around **30 days with a 7-day grace** and adjust after a month. You can
change both at any time, up to 365 days each.

An interval and grace of zero are allowed, but the estate is then claimable right away.
That is useful for testing and almost never what you want.

## After a guardian pause

A check-in during a guardian's pause works as normal but leaves the pause in place. The
first check-in after the pause ends clears it, which lets the guardian pause again if
they ever need to.
