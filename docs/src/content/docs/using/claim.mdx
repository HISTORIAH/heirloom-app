If you have been named as an heir, this is the only page you need. You don't need the
owner's address, a document, or a password.

## What you need

- The wallet the owner named. Holding it is what entitles you.
- A little SOL for network fees.

## How to claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inherit page">
    [app.heirlm.xyz/inherit](https://app.heirlm.xyz/inherit), with your wallet connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your estates">
    The page lists every estate that names your wallet, with its state and what it
    holds. There is nothing to enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Pick the estate and sign once. Every asset is sent to your wallet and the estate
    closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you can claim

Once the owner's last check-in, plus their check-in interval, plus their grace period,
has passed. If a guardian paused the estate, you also have to wait for the pause to end.
The Inherit page shows the countdown.

There is no rush. A claimable estate stays claimable until someone acts. But until you
claim, the owner can still close the estate and take everything back, so if it matters,
claim it.

## What you receive

Every asset in the estate, minus a **0.75%** fee each. An estate holding 10 SOL and 5,000
USDC pays out 9.925 SOL and 4,962.5 USDC. Token accounts you don't have yet are created
for you.

## If it fails

| Error | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `NotYetClaimable` | The deadline hasn't passed, or a pause is still running | Wait for the countdown |
| `FundsStillDeployed` | An asset is in a yield position | Only the owner can recall it |
| `InsufficientVaultBalance` | Nothing left for that asset | It was already claimed or the estate was closed |

<Callout type="danger" title="Funds in yield are stuck until the owner acts">
If the owner left assets in a Lulo position, only the owner's wallet can bring them
back. Owners: recall your positions before you stop using an estate actively.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="If you are an owner">
This is everything your heir will face. The one thing they can't find out for
themselves is that Heirloom exists and that they were named. Tell them.
</Callout>
