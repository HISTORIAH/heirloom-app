If you have been named as an heir, this is the only page you need. You do not need the
owner's address, a document, a password, or anything they would have had to give you in
advance.

## What you need

- The wallet whose address the owner named. That wallet is the entitlement.
- A little SOL for network fees.
- Enough time to have passed.

## How to claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the claim page">
    [app.heirlm.xyz/claim](https://app.heirlm.xyz/claim), with your wallet connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it find you">
    The page scans the program for every estate whose `heir` field is your address. You
    do not enter anything — if an estate names you, it appears, along with its state and
    what it holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Pick the estate and sign once. The transaction pays out every registered asset:
    tokens first, then SOL, which closes the vault and returns its rent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the program will allow it

<Code caption="programs/heirloom/src/instructions/claim.rs">
```rust
let claimable_at = estate.last_heartbeat
    + estate.heartbeat_interval
    + estate.grace_period;

require!(now >= claimable_at.max(estate.paused_until), NotYetClaimable);
```
</Code>

Before that moment, a claim fails with `NotYetClaimable` — there is no partial state, no
queue, and no way to register intent early. After it, the estate stays claimable
indefinitely; nothing expires and nothing is forfeited by waiting.

## What arrives

Every registered asset, minus a **0.75%** protocol fee each. If the estate held 10 SOL
and 5,000 USDC, you receive 9.925 SOL and 4,962.5 USDC.

Token accounts are created for you if you do not already have one for a mint.

## What can stop it

| Error | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `NotYetClaimable` | The deadline has not passed, or a guardian's pause is still live | Wait. The page shows the countdown |
| `FundsStillDeployed` | An asset is out in a yield strategy | Only the owner can recall it. See below |
| `TokensFirst` | The SOL leg was attempted while token accounts were still open | Use the app, which orders them correctly |
| `InsufficientVaultBalance` | The vault is empty for that asset | It was already claimed or withdrawn |

<Callout type="danger" title="Deployed funds are a real dead end">
If an asset is sitting in a Lulo position when the owner goes quiet, only the authority
can recall it — the heir has no instruction that can. Those funds stay in the strategy
until the authority's key acts. If you are an owner reading this: recall your positions
before you stop using the estate actively.
</Callout>

## The owner can still take it back

Until you sign, the owner's emergency withdraw works, even while the estate is claimable.
Whoever signs first wins. If the estate matters and it is claimable, claim it.

## After the claim

The estate and vault accounts are closed. The arrangement is over, and nothing further is
owed to you or by you. The assets are in your wallet, held by your keys.

<Callout type="tip" title="If you are an owner reading this page">
Everything above is what your heir will face. The one thing they cannot work out for
themselves is that Heirloom exists and that they were named. Tell them, and tell them
nothing else is required — no seed phrase, no address, no paperwork.
</Callout>
