A guardian is for when the owner is fine but can't check in: in hospital, off-grid, or
locked out of a device. The guardian can **pause the deadline once**, pushing it out by
the pause length the owner chose. That is all they can do.

## How to pause

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pause page">
    [app.heirlm.xyz/defer](https://app.heirlm.xyz/defer), with the guardian wallet
    connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the estate">
    Enter the owner's address and the heir's address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    The deadline moves to now plus the pause length. Until then, the heir can't claim,
    even if the grace period has already run out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When it works

- **Before the estate is claimable.** Once the heir can claim, it is too late and the
  pause fails with `DeferWindowExpired`.
- **Once.** A second pause fails with `AlreadyDeferred`. The guardian can pause again
  only after the pause has ended and the owner has checked in.

## What a guardian can't do

Move or see funds, change the heir or the timing, claim, pause twice, or stop the owner
doing anything. The worst a hostile guardian can do is delay the inheritance by one
pause length.

## Choosing a guardian

Pick someone who would notice you had gone quiet and would take the time to sign. They
need a Solana wallet with a few cents for fees, and nothing else. The guardian is set
when the estate is created and can't be changed yet.
