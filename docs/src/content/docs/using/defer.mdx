A guardian — `delegate` on the estate — exists for one situation: the owner is fine but
unreachable. In hospital, off-grid, mid-move, locked out of a device. The check-ins have
stopped and the estate is drifting toward a handover nobody wants yet.

The guardian can push the deadline out. That is all they can do.

## How to defer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the defer page">
    [app.heirlm.xyz/defer](https://app.heirlm.xyz/defer), with the guardian wallet
    connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the pair">
    The owner's address and the heir's address — those two derive the estate PDA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    One transaction. `paused_until` is set to `now + pause_duration`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it does, exactly

<Code caption="programs/heirloom/src/instructions/defer.rs">
```rust
estate.paused_until = now + estate.pause_duration;
```
</Code>

`paused_until` acts as a floor on the claim time: the estate is claimable at
`max(claimable_at, paused_until)`. So even if the grace period ran out days ago, a live
pause keeps the heir out until it expires.

## Two conditions

<Code caption="The validate step">
```rust
require!(estate.paused_until == 0, AlreadyDeferred);
require!(now < claimable_at,       DeferWindowExpired);
```
</Code>

- **Once per estate.** `paused_until` has to be zero on entry. After a deferral it stays
  non-zero until the owner returns and checks in *after* it expires, which is what resets
  it to zero and frees the guardian to act again.
- **Before the deadline.** A deferral is only accepted while the estate is still active or
  in grace. Once it is claimable, it is too late — `DeferWindowExpired`.

<Callout type="warning" title="A guardian is a one-shot, not a subscription">
The window closes at `claimable_at`, not at the end of the grace period, so a guardian has
the whole interval plus the grace period to act. Once the estate is claimable, no power on
the estate can pull it back except the owner's own withdrawal.
</Callout>

## What a guardian cannot do

- Move, spend or see any funds
- Change the heir, the durations, or the label
- Claim anything
- Defer twice
- Stop the owner from doing anything at all

The worst a hostile guardian can do is delay an inheritance by exactly the
`pause_duration` the owner chose — once.

## Choosing one

Pick someone who would notice you were unreachable and could be bothered to sign a
transaction. They need a Solana wallet and nothing else: no funds beyond a few cents of
network fees, no ongoing role, and no knowledge of your keys.

Naming a guardian is set at creation and cannot be changed on a live estate — see
[Roles & permissions](/docs/concepts/roles/#the-matrix).
