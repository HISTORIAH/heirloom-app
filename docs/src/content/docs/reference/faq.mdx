### Is this a legal will?

No. Heirloom moves control of on-chain assets from one wallet to another under a
condition the chain can check. It isn't filed anywhere and doesn't interact with probate.
Talk to a lawyer about your will.

### Do I have to give anyone my seed phrase?

Never. Not your heir, not your guardian, not Heirloom.

### Can Heirloom take my assets?

No. There is no admin instruction, and the treasury only receives fees. See
[Security & trust](/docs/reference/security/).

### What if Heirloom the company disappears?

The program stays on-chain and keeps working. The source is public and every
instruction can be built without the app.

### Can I have more than one heir?

Not in one estate. Open one estate per heir. See
[How it works](/docs/how-it-works/#one-estate-one-heir).

### Can I change my heir, guardian or check-in signer?

The heir, yes: see [Change the heir](/docs/using/change-heir/). The guardian and
check-in signer can't be changed yet. See [How it works](/docs/how-it-works/#who-can-do-what).

### What if I forget to check in?

The estate enters its grace period and the app warns you. A check-in at any point before
grace runs out resets everything. See [Check in](/docs/using/check-in/).

### Does adding assets count as a check-in?

No. Only a check-in or a timing change does.

### Can I get my assets back?

Any time before your heir claims, by [closing the estate](/docs/using/close-estate/), for
a 0.5% fee.

### What if my heir and I both act at once?

Whichever transaction lands first wins, and the other fails.

### Does anything happen automatically when the deadline passes?

No. The estate waits until your heir claims it.

### What does it cost?

Opening, adding assets and checking in are free apart from network fees. See
[Fees](/docs/fees/).

### Why does my estate show slightly less than I put in?

The app leaves out the small rent reserve every Solana account holds. You get it back
when the estate closes.

### Which tokens can I use?

SOL and SPL tokens, including Token-2022. Not NFTs. See [Fund an estate](/docs/using/fund-estate/#what-you-can-add).

### Is it on mainnet?

Not yet. It is audited and on devnet.

### Can I use it to recover my own wallet?

Yes. See [Wallet recovery](/docs/using/wallet-recovery/).

### Is any of this private?

No. Everything on-chain is public. See [Security & trust](/docs/reference/security/#what-is-public).
