## What the program guarantees

- **No admin.** No privileged instruction, no pause switch, and no address outside the
  estate's own roles that can move funds.
- **No keys to lose.** Every estate account is program-derived. No private key exists
  for any of them.
- **No custody.** Assets sit in accounts only the program can sign for.
- **Fixed fees.** The rates and the treasury address are constants. Changing them means
  deploying a new program.
- **A predictable deadline.** An heir can claim exactly when the deadline passes, and
  never before.

The program is deployed to devnet and has been independently audited.

## What it doesn't guarantee

- **Token issuers' powers.** A token whose issuer can freeze accounts or add a transfer
  hook can still be blocked by that issuer. See [Fund an estate](/docs/using/fund-estate/#tokens-that-need-care).
- **Funds left in yield.** Assets in a Lulo position can't be claimed, and only the owner
  can recall them. See [Earn yield](/docs/using/yield/).
- **Lulo's risk.** While a position is open you carry Lulo's risk, including its exposure
  to Kamino. Heirloom doesn't insure it.
- **Your heir finding out.** Nothing happens automatically and nothing tells your heir.
  If they don't know Heirloom exists, the estate just waits.

## What is public

Everything on-chain: your address, your heir's, your guardian's, your timing, and every
balance and transaction. Anyone can read and connect them. That is how Solana works.

## What the app collects

Analytics are off unless turned on at build time. When on, they record page views and
clicks only. They never send wallet addresses, heir addresses, transaction signatures,
amounts, form values or error messages. Captured text is masked and session recording
is off.

## Good practice

- **Use a check-in signer** so the owner key can stay on a hardware wallet.
- **Name a guardian.** The worst a hostile one can do is delay by one pause length.
- **Check the heir address.** There is no undo. Send a small amount first.
- **Recall yield before you go quiet.** It is the one thing your heir can't fix.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hey@heirlm.xyz** or DM **@heirloom_app**. The same contact is published
on-chain in the program's `security_txt`. Please don't open a public issue for anything
that could put funds at risk.
