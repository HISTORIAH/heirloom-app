An inheritance plan that relies on you remembering a date is a plan with a hole in it. Starting today, Heirloom can remind you before your check-in is due, and let your heir know the moment an estate becomes claimable. Telegram is the first channel, with more to follow.

## Why reminders

Heirloom works on a simple rule: as long as you keep checking in, nothing happens. Miss a check-in and the countdown to your heir's claim begins.

That rule is only fair if you actually know the deadline is coming. Check-in intervals are measured in months, and nobody keeps a months-away date in their head. Reminders were the most requested feature we had.

## What's new

### Reminders for you

Turn on reminders for an estate and we'll message you:

- **7 days before** your check-in is due
- **When the countdown elapses**
- **When the grace period starts**
- **Halfway through** the grace period
- **24 hours before** the grace period ends

Check in at any point and the remaining reminders for that cycle are cancelled. The same goes if you pause or close the estate.

### A message for your heir

Your heir doesn't need to watch a dashboard or know what a grace period is. Once the estate is claimable, they get a message telling them so, with instructions on how to claim. If the estate stays unclaimed, we follow up.

### Telegram first

We're launching with Telegram. Telegram bots can't message someone who hasn't started them, and we think that's the right default. Every contact is verified through a one-tap link that opens our bot. Until it's tapped, nothing is sent. If the link expires or gets lost, you can resend it from the dashboard.

## How to turn it on

1. Open the [app](https://app.heirlm.xyz) and find your estate on the dashboard
2. Under **Notifications**, click **Set up**
3. Sign the message to prove you own the wallet. No transaction, no gas
4. Choose **Telegram** and enter your username
5. Save, then tap **Open Telegram** and press **Start** in our bot

To notify your heir, add their Telegram username in the same dialog. They'll need to open the verification link and press **Start** too, so send it to them after you save.

## Your contact details

We held off on reminders for a long time because it meant storing contact details, and we've all seen what happens when a crypto product leaks its customer list. So we built this with as little exposure as we could:

- **Opt-in, per estate.** Nothing is collected unless you turn reminders on.
- **Encrypted at rest.** Contact details are only decrypted at the moment a reminder is sent.
- **Wallet-gated.** Viewing or changing notification settings requires a signature from the estate owner's wallet, valid for that session only.
- **Offchain by design.** Your contact details never touch the chain.

If you want the engineering details, we wrote about the reminder pipeline in [Rust Background Jobs with Apalis and Postgres](/blog/apalis-reminder-workers-solana/).

## What's next

Telegram is the first channel, not the last. Email and Discord are next, and the schedule above will work the same way on each. If there's a channel you'd like us to prioritise, tell us.

Reminders are a safety net, not a replacement for checking in. But a safety net is exactly what a plan like this should have. [Set yours up](https://app.heirlm.xyz) today.
